# Google Play Console – Verification & Launch Checklist

## Account
- [ ] Choose account type (Personal / Organization) – cannot be changed later
- [ ] Pay one-time registration fee
- [ ] Verify phone number (code)
- [ ] Verify contact email
- [ ] Public support email set on developer profile
- [ ] Upload government ID
- [ ] (Org only) D-U-N-S number, public phone, organization document
- [ ] (Personal) Complete closed-testing requirement per current Play rules

## App assets
- [ ] Icon 512x512
- [ ] Feature graphic 1024x500
- [ ] 2+ phone screenshots
- [ ] Signed .aab
- [ ] Privacy policy published at a public URL (see PRIVACY_POLICY.md)
- [ ] Data safety form
- [ ] Content rating questionnaire
- [ ] Store listing text (see LISTING.md)
